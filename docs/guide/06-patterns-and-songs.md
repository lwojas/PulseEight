# Patterns & songs

## Patterns

Pulse.8 has **64 patterns**. A pattern is everything about a passage of music:

- the steps of all eight tracks, 16 – 64 steps long
- every track's sound, level, delay and reverb
- the Punch FX character knobs, sweep time and written FX lane

Because the sound travels with the pattern, switching pattern can switch the entire sound of the groove.

On a wide screen the pattern bar shows one bank of 16 at a time (1–16, 17–32, 33–48, 49–64). On a phone, open the
**PTN** tab.

### Switching patterns

- **Stopped:** tapping a slot selects it at once.
- **Playing:** tapping a slot **cues** it. The header shows `PTN → 05` and the switch happens at the end of the current
  loop, on the beat, with the new sound applied sample-accurately. Tapping the pattern that is playing cancels the cue.

### Tools

| Button | Does |
|---|---|
| **COPY** | Tap COPY, then tap a slot: the current pattern is copied there, overwriting it. Tap the current slot to cancel. |
| **CLEAR** | Empties the current pattern (tap twice). |

## Chains

A **chain** plays patterns one after another: a song.

1. Tap **EDIT** under CHAIN.
2. Tap pattern slots in order. Each tap appends to the chain. A chain holds up to 64 entries and can repeat patterns.
3. Tap **DONE**, then switch the chain **ON**.

Tap an entry in the chain to remove it. **CLR** empties the chain. With the chain on, playback starts at its first
entry and loops through it. Cue a pattern that is in the chain and the chain continues from that position.

The chain, and whether it is on, is saved with the project.

## Building a track

1. **Pattern 1:** the main groove.
2. **COPY** it to pattern 2. Strip the kick and let the FM line carry it: a breakdown.
3. **COPY** the main groove to pattern 3. Load the **TECHNO HARD** kit and write a **ROLL** into the last bar of the
   lane: a build.
4. Chain them: `1 1 1 1 2 2 3 1 1 1 1`.
